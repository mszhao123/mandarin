Paper 1 Exam Mastery: Text Types (文本类型)

写 (Xiě) • Senior Paper 1 Text Formats

In Paper 1, seniors choose 1 prompt out of 3 options (250–400 words). Format adherence and formal discourse are critical for Criterion B!

Key Paper 1 Formats & Conventions

1. 议论文 (Opinion Essay) Clear thesis, pros/cons counterarguments, evidence, logical conclusion.
2. 建议书 / 提议书 (Proposal) Recipient header, clear objectives, actionable suggestions, formal sign-off.
3. 采访记 / 访谈录 (Interview) Interviewee intro, Q&A layout (问/答), reflective closing summary.
4. 演讲稿 (Formal Speech) Audience greeting, rhetorical questions, persuasive call to action, formal closing.

📋 External & Internal Assessment Structure

  • Paper 1 (25%): External Writing (250–400 words) using proper text formats (议论文, 建议书, 采访记, 演讲稿, etc.).
  • Paper 2 (50%): External Receptive Skills (Reading & Listening comprehension on global themes).
  • Individual Oral IA (25%): Internal Spoken Assessment based on visual stimulus + global theme discussion.

🎯 Senior Graduation Expectations

High-level formal register, analytical precision, fluent transition structures, active participation in mock examinations, and consistent preparation for final IB DP May exams.
