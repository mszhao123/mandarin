Pillar 2: Learn By Writing

写 (Xiě) • Stroke Rules & Tian Zi Ge Pad

In Grade 8, proper character proportion and stroke order are crucial. Practice writing essential characters step-by-step on our digital canvas!

Golden Rules of Character Writing

1. Top to Bottom (从上到下) e.g., 言 (yán - speech)
2. Left to Right (从左到右) e.g., 好 (hǎo - good)
3. Horizontal then Vertical e.g., 十 (shí - ten)
4. Close Frame Last e.g., 国 (guó - country)

Pillar 3: Learn By Listening

听 (Tīng) • The 4 Mandarin Tones

In Grade 8, tuning your ear to subtle pitch changes is essential for listening comprehension and accuracy.

1st Tone (55)

mā (妈)

High & Level Pitch

Constant high musical note tone.

2nd Tone (35)

má (麻)

Rising Pitch

Rising question intonation tone.

3rd Tone (214)

mǎ (马)

Dipping & Rising

Low dipping tone valley.

4th Tone (51)

mà (骂)

Sharp Falling Pitch

Sharp emphatic dropping tone.

Pillar 4: Learn By Reading & Culture

读 (Dú) • Radicals & Cultural Roots

Understanding radicals allows 8th graders to unlock character meanings without relying solely on Pinyin!

讠

Speech Radical (yán)

Characters with 讠 always relate to speaking, language, or words!

Examples: 说 (speak), 语 (language), 话 (speech)
氵

Three-Drops Water Radical (shuǐ)

Characters with 氵 relate to water, liquids, or body of waters!

Examples: 河 (river), 海 (ocean), 洗 (wash)
日

Sun / Time Radical (rì)

Characters with 日 represent sunlight, time of day, or brightness!

Examples: 明 (bright/tomorrow), 早 (early), 晴 (sunny)
