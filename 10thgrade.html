Pillar 2: Learn By Writing

写 (Xiě) • High School Character Practice

Chinese characters are constructed following universal stroke order rules. Master these structural fundamentals on our digital Tian Zi Ge canvas!

Golden Rules of Character Writing

1. Top to Bottom (从上到下) e.g., 三 (sān - three)
2. Left to Right (从左到右) e.g., 川 (chuān - river)
3. Horizontal then Vertical e.g., 十 (shí - ten)
4. Outside then Inside e.g., 国 (guó - country)

1st Tone (55)

Pillar 4: Learn By Reading & Culture

读 (Dú) • High School Radicals & Contexts

Chinese radicals provide semantic clues! Unlock meaning in complex high school literature through structural keys.

讠

Speech Radical (yán / 言)

When you see 讠 in a character, it relates to language, speech, or communication!

Examples: 语 (language), 话 (speech), 说 (talk)
氵

Three-Dot Water Radical (shuǐ / 水)

When 氵 appears on the left, it signifies liquids, rivers, or water elements.

Examples: 河 (river), 海 (ocean), 洗 (wash)
宀

Roof Radical (bǎogǎitóu)

Characters featuring 宀 usually relate to houses, shelter, or indoor spaces.

Examples: 家 (family/home), 安 (peace), 室 (room)
