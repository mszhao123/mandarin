Core Curriculum Architecture

The 5 IB DP Themes (五大核心主题)

All assessments, readings, and oral discussions in Grade 11 and 12 revolve around these global perspectives.

身
Theme 01

Identities (身份认同)

Lifestyles, health, beliefs, language, identity, and personal values.

体
Theme 02

Experiences (体验)

Leisure activities, travel, life stories, rites of passage, and customs.

创
Theme 03

Human Ingenuity (人类创造)

Technology, scientific innovation, artistic expression, media, and communication.

社
Theme 04

Social Organization (社会组织)

Education, workplace, law & order, community, and social structures.

球
Theme 05

Sharing the Planet (共享地球)

Environment, climate change, globalization, ethics, and human rights.

DP Oral & Argumentation Connectors

说 (Shuō) • Academic Discourse & Expressing Opinions

Click any phrase to hear formal discourse connectors essential for your IB Individual Oral (IO)!

Audio Speed:
在我看来...

Zài wǒ kàn lái...

In my opinion / From my perspective...

💡 Essential starter for presenting arguments in DP IO.
从...的角度来看

Cóng ... de jiǎodù lái kàn

From the perspective of...

💡 Use for multi-angled analysis (e.g., environment, culture).
一方面...另一方面...

Yì fāngmiàn... lìng yì fāngmiàn...

On one hand... on the other hand...

💡 Perfect connector for showing balanced evaluation.
不可否认的是

Bù kě fǒurèn de shì

Undeniably / It cannot be denied that...

💡 High-level vocabulary for essay and oral argumentation.
总而言之

Zǒng ér yán zhī

In conclusion / All in all...

💡 Use to wrap up oral conclusions or Paper 1 essays.
这幅图片展现了...

Zhè fú túpiàn zhǎnxiàn le...

This picture illustrates / demonstrates...

💡 Directly applicable to SL Picture Description during IO.
Paper 1 Mastery: Text Types (文本类型)

写 (Xiě) • Text Types & Advanced Writing

In IB DP Paper 1, you must select the correct format, register, and tone. Practice high-frequency academic vocabulary and character structures below!

Key DP Text Types (Paper 1 Expectations)

1. Blog / VLOG Script (博客/视频脚本) Informal/semi-formal, personal voice.
2. Formal Speech / Presentation (演讲稿) Greeting, audience awareness, rhetorical structure.
3. Official / Formal Letter (公函/信件) Formal opening, polite address, concluding formula.
4. Article / Report (文章/新闻报道) Catchy headline, structured paragraphs, subheadings.

Course Syllabus

IB DP Chinese B (SL/HL) Course Overview

Josiah Quincy Upper School • Instructor: Ms. Zhao

📋 Assessment Structure

  • Paper 1 (25%): Writing assessment (choose 1 text type from 3 prompts).
  • Paper 2 (50%): Receptive skills (Listening & Reading comprehension).
  • Individual Oral IA (25%): Oral presentation based on an IB picture prompt / literary extract + discussion.

🎯 Academic Expectations

Consistent engagement with authentic texts, daily vocabulary retention, active participation in class debates, and independent reading of Chinese media and IB DP core themes.
