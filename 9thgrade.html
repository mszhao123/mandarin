Speech Speed:
很高兴认识你

Nice to meet you!

💡 High school self-introduction essential phrase!
我是九年级的学生

Wǒ shì jiǔ niánjí de xuésheng

I am a 9th grade student.

💡 "九年级" = 9th grade / freshman year!
请问，这句话什么意思？

Qǐngwèn, zhè jù huà shénme yìsi?

Excuse me, what does this sentence mean?

💡 Key classroom clarification question to ask Ms. Zhao!
我的目标是学好中文

Wǒ de mùbiāo shì xué hǎo zhōngwén

My goal is to master Chinese.

💡 "目标" (mùbiāo) = goal / objective.
我们一起加油！

Wǒmen yìqǐ jiāyóu!

Let's work hard together!

💡 "加油" (jiāyóu) = encouragement ("add fuel/go for it!").
祝你今天过得愉快！

Zhù nǐ jīntiān guò de yúkuài!

Have a wonderful day!

💡 Politeness phrase for greeting teachers and peers.

Pillar 3: Tone Pair Mastery (听力与辨析)

9th Grade Tone Combination Ear Trainer

In high school Mandarin, single tones combine into **Tone Pairs** (e.g. 1st + 4th tone). Train your ear to recognize these continuous pitch flows!

1st + 2nd Tone

中国 (Zhōngguó)

High Level → Rising Pitch

Meaning: China

3rd + 1st Tone

老师 (Lǎoshī)

Low Dip → High Level Pitch

Meaning: Teacher

4th + 4th Tone

再见 (Zàijiàn)

Falling → Sharp Falling

Meaning: Goodbye

3rd + 1st Tone

喜欢 (Xǐhuān)

Dipping → High Level

Meaning: To Like
